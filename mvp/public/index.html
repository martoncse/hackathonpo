✓ CatatSekali “record once” in Indonesian

A visit that never reaches P-Care
doesn't count.

Indonesian community health centres still record outreach, posyandu and maternal visits on paper, or miss them when the internet or P-Care is down. BPJS pays part of each centre's capitation on its contact rate, counted only from P-Care. CatatSekali reads the paper sheet, finds the visits P-Care is missing, shows what they're worth, and prepares them to send.

Rule: Peraturan BPJS Kesehatan 7/2019. Burden: staff in about one centre in five enter data into more than six programmes (Ministry of Health survey, 2022 data, JMIR Medical Informatics 2025). Full map in ECOSYSTEM.md.
