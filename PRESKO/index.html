How the rule works

Claude only reads building notes into the features below. The points and bands are fixed and visible. Nothing estimates a room temperature.

Planner: if every section fits its usual shift in green rooms, the school runs a full day. Otherwise sections move to green and amber rooms in the morning (one 06:00–11:00 slot, or two 2.5-hour slots). Sections that still don't fit go to alternative delivery. A separate checker then re-reads every assignment.

What's real, what isn't

  • Real: 70 public schools mapped in OpenStreetMap inside Iloilo City (HOT export on HDX). OpenStreetMap may miss some schools.
  • Real: Iloilo's daily "feels-like" maximum in April 2024 from the Open-Meteo archive. It is a proxy and runs lower than PAGASA's heat index. On the day, the coordinator types PAGASA's forecast.
  • Real: PAGASA heat-index categories: caution 27–32°C, extreme caution 33–41°C, danger 42–51°C, extreme danger 52°C and above (Manila Bulletin).
  • Real: DepEd suspended in-person classes in all public schools on 29–30 April 2024 (Rappler). By 25 April, 7,188 of 47,678 public schools had already suspended in-person classes (Manila Bulletin).
  • Real: heatwaves disrupted schooling for 171 million students in 2024 (UNICEF).
  • Illustrative: building notes, room counts, sections, shifts and learner numbers. The division fills in its own.
  • Morning-only is not free: it cuts in-person time to 5 hours or less, and alternative delivery assumes modules or a connection at home.
