## 1. School suspensions, ADM shifts and memos (April 2024)

### Takeaway
Iloilo City's LGU cancelled in-person classes at all basic-education levels on April 1–5, 2024 (EO 047 and its amendments) and again on April 25–26, 2024. On April 1, 66 Iloilo City public schools were among more than 800 suspending in Western Visayas, and Region VI led the country in ADM shifts all through April–May. No SDO Iloilo City or DepEd RO VI memo number was found. The suspensions came from city executive orders plus DepEd's national rule that school heads decide.

### Cited Findings
**Iloilo City: executive orders and dates**
- Mayor Jerry Treñas signed the first order on March 31, 2024. It suspended in-person classes from preschool to SHS, public and private, on April 1–2, citing a PAGASA "danger" heat index forecast of 41°C. — [GMA](https://gmanetwork.com/news/serbisyopubliko/walangpasok/902179/iloilo-city-suspends-face-to-face-classes-for-april-1-and-2-2024/story) [snippet]; [GMA Regional TV](https://gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/101176/no-inperson-classes-in-iloilo-city-from-preschool-to-shs-on-april-1-and-2/story) [snippet]
- EO No. 047 (s. 2024) suspended F2F classes from preschool to SHS starting Monday, April 1. EO No. 047-B, effective Wednesday April 3, extended the suspension and widened it to all public and private institutions, including colleges and graduate school. Schools with enough air-conditioning could continue F2F classes at the discretion of their heads. PAGASA's 5 p.m. April 2 forecast was 41°C for April 3 and 42°C for April 4 (the article says "temperatures", but these are almost certainly heat index values). ## 2. PAGASA heat index for Iloilo City (late April 2024) vs. prototype proxy

### Takeaway
PAGASA put Iloilo City in its "danger" band (42–51°C) several times in April–May 2024: 43°C on April 12, 43°C forecast for April 23, a 43°C forecast that drove the April 25–26 suspension, and a 44°C peak on May 17. No PAGASA value for April 29 or 30 was found. The prototype's Open-Meteo apparent_temperature_max (37.2°C on Apr 29, 39.4°C on Apr 30) sits 4–6°C below the PAGASA figures for nearby days, so it very likely underestimates.

### Cited Findings
- PAGASA danger band: heat index 42–51°C, where heat cramps and exhaustion are likely and heat stroke is probable with continued exposure. — [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1936518/heat-index-in-ph-continues-to-peak-at-dangerous-levels-warns-pagasa/amp) [snippet]
- Iloilo City at 42°C on March 30, 2024. — [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2024/3/30/pagasa-records-dangerous-heat-indices-in-6-areas-on-march-30-1) [snippet]
- April 2 forecast for April 3–4: 41°C and 42°C. — [Daily Guardian](https://www.dailyguardian.com.ph/blog/iloilo-city-extends-f2f-class-halts-includes-colleges)
- Iloilo City at 43°C on April 12, 2024. — [search summary of PAGASA list coverage](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1929364/heat-index-reaches-danger-category-in-6-areas-on-friday) [snippet; date-to-article mapping UNCONFIRMED]
- Dumangas, Iloilo at 43°C on April 17. — [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1930636/heat-index-april-17-42c-to-44c-temperature-in-17-areas) [snippet]
- April 23, 2024 forecast: Iloilo City 43°C, Dumangas 43°C, Mambusao (Capiz) 42°C. — [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/scitech/weather/904408/dangerous-heat-index-expected-in-25-areas-on-april-23/story). A search summary gave 44°C for Iloilo City around April 23, which conflicts with GMA's 43°C. Use 43°C.
- April 25–26 forecast: 43°C for Iloilo City (basis of the suspension). — [Manila Times coverage](https://manilatimes.net/2024/04/26/regions/classes-halted-anew-in-iloilo-city-schools/1943313/amp) [snippet]
- May 17, 2024: heat index peaked at 44°C at NAIA and Iloilo City. — [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2024/5/17/heat-index-in-35-areas-reaches-danger-level-on-may-17) [snippet]
- National 2024 high: 53°C in Iba, Zambales on April 28, 2024. — [PAGASA heat index summary PDF](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/tamss/weather/heat_index_summary.pdf); [ABS-CBN, Apr 29, 2024](https://abs-cbn.com/regions/2024/4/29/iba-zambales-hits-53-c-heat-index-36-areas-face-danger-heat-levels-950) [snippets]

### Inferences
- Comparison: Open-Meteo apparent_temperature_max of 37.2°C (Apr 29) and 39.4°C (Apr 30) against PAGASA's 43°C (Apr 23, Apr 25–26) means the proxy reads about 4–6°C low. PAGASA uses the NOAA/Rothfusz heat index on station temperature and humidity. Open-Meteo's apparent temperature is a different (Steadman-type) formula on gridded reanalysis or forecast data, and it includes wind. The two are not interchangeable. The demo should label the proxy as "may underestimate PAGASA heat index by several °C", or calibrate it (for example, add about 4°C or compute a Rothfusz heat index from Open-Meteo temperature_2m and relative_humidity_2m).
- At the city's 2025 thresholds (40°C optional, 41–42°C automatic), the raw proxy values would *not* have triggered a suspension on April 29–30, while PAGASA-level values on nearby days did.

### Gaps
- No PAGASA Iloilo station value (observed or forecast) for April 29 or 30, 2024. PAGASA's daily heat index bulletins or a TAMSS data request would settle this.
- The mid-April readings (Apr 12, Apr 17) come from search summaries, not full-article reads.

## 3. LGU class suspensions and executive orders, 2024–2025

### Takeaway
Iloilo City used executive orders in 2024 (EO 047 series) and then made the rules standing in 2025. EO No. 24 (effective March 9, 2025) sets a 40°C optional and 42°C automatic heat index threshold, keyed to PAGASA's 5 p.m. advisory the day before. The automatic threshold was reportedly lowered to 41°C, and it was used on April 7, 2025.

### Cited Findings
- 2024: EO 047, 047-B (Apr 3) and 047-D (Apr 5), plus the April 25–26 suspension (see section 1). — [Daily Guardian](https://www.dailyguardian.com.ph/blog/iloilo-city-extends-f2f-class-halts-includes-colleges); [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/iloilo/treñas-extends-suspension-of-face-to-face-classes-in-iloilo-city-due-to-extreme-heat-conditions) [snippet]
- Other WV LGUs that suspended in early April 2024: Bacolod City, Roxas City, E.B. Magalona, Hinoba-an and Talisay City (to April 2), with Capiz and Negros Occidental also. — [GMA Regional TV](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/101185/over-800-schools-in-western-visayas-suspend-inperson-classes/story); [Inquirer: "4 West Visayas mayors suspend classes"](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1924817/4-west-visayas-mayors-suspend-classes-amid-intense-heat/amp) [title]
- **EO No. 24 (Iloilo City, signed by Mayor Treñas, effective March 9, 2025):**
  - At a 40°C forecast heat index, suspension is optional and left to school heads, administrators and university presidents.
  - At 42°C, F2F classes are automatically suspended and move to ADM.
  - The trigger is PAGASA's 5 p.m. heat index advisory the day before a school day. The CDRRMO recommended the 40°C figure.
  - Schools may start early so classes end by noon, with afternoon classes moved to ADM.
  - Source: [GMA Regional TV, Mar 10, 2025](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/106983/new-protocols-issued-on-class-suspension-in-iloilo-city-due-to-heat-index/story)
- **April 7, 2025:** in-person classes from preschool to SHS in Iloilo City were suspended on a 41°C PAGASA forecast. The automatic-suspension threshold had been "lowered" from 42°C to 41°C, and tertiary schools were left to their own discretion. SDO Iloilo City said some schools recalled students for urgent activities with parental consent. 123 public schools in Western Visayas cancelled classes that day. — [GMA Regional TV, Apr 7, 2025](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/107479/iloilo-city-cancels-inperson-classes-due-to-41c-heat-index/story)

### Inferences
- EO 24's structure (PAGASA's 5 p.m. forecast the day before, an optional band and an automatic threshold, a half-day option) maps directly onto a plan-ahead tool like Presko. The prototype could adopt these exact thresholds (40 / 41–42°C) as the local rule set.

### Gaps
- Which instrument lowered the threshold to 41°C (an amended EO number) is not identified.
- Other 2025 suspension dates in Iloilo City beyond April 7 were not found.

## 6. Language: Hiligaynon and RA 12027

### Takeaway
Hiligaynon (Ilonggo) is the main language of Iloilo and Western Visayas. RA 12027 (October 2024) ended mandatory mother-tongue medium of instruction for K–3: instruction reverts to Filipino and English, with mother tongue optional for monolingual classes. DepEd-6 said teachers would still use the mother tongue to explain lessons.

### Cited Findings
- Hiligaynon is concentrated in Western Visayas (Iloilo, Capiz, Guimaras, Negros Occidental). — [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Ilongo_language)
- RA 12027 lapsed into law on about October 10–11, 2024. The medium of instruction reverts to Filipino and English, with regional languages as auxiliary media. Mother tongue stays optional for monolingual classes, and DepEd and KWF must do language mapping. — [LawPhil RA 12027](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_12027_2024.html); [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1992046/use-of-mother-tongue-as-mode-of-instruction-ends-as-bill-lapses-into-law/amp)
- The IRR was issued May 2025 and disseminated through DM 059, s. 2025 (August 1, 2025). — [DepEd IRR](https://www.deped.gov.ph/2025/05/28/implementing-rules-and-regulations-of-republic-act-no-12027-titled-an-act-discontinuing-the-use-of-the-mother-tongue-as-medium-of-instruction-from-kindergarten-to-grade-3-providing-for-its-optional/); [DepEd DM 059](https://www.deped.gov.ph/2025/08/01/august-1-2025-dm-059-s-2025-dissemination-of-implementing-rules-and-regulations-irr-of-republic-act-ra-no-12027-titled-an-act-discontinuing-the-use-of-the-mother-tongue-as-medium-of/)
- DepEd-6's Dr. Elena Gonzaga said teachers still need to use the mother tongue, especially to explain hard Filipino and English terms. — [Panay News](https://www.panaynews.net/use-of-mother-tongue-in-classrooms-seen-to-persist-despite-new-law-discontinuing-it) [snippet]
- Before RA 12027, Iloilo City schools used Hiligaynon MTB-MLE materials. USAID/RTI distributed Hiligaynon books to Iloilo City schools in 2021. — [Manila Bulletin, May 4, 2021](https://mb.com.ph/2021/05/04/usaid-rti-release-hiligaynon-written-books-to-iloilo-city-schools/)

### Inferences
- Parent-facing messages in Presko should be in Hiligaynon (with English and Filipino), since that is the home language. Official DepEd communications may be in English or Filipino.

### Gaps
- No Iloilo City-specific post-RA 12027 language policy was found.
