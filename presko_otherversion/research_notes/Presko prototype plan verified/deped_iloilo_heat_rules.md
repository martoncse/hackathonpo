## Q1. DepEd Memorandum No. 064, s. 2026: the 4H levels, thresholds, decision-maker and timing

### Takeaway
DM 064 s.2026 was released on 26 Oct 2026 and signed by Sec. Sonny Angara. It takes effect immediately and lasts until PAGASA's final advisory that the 2026–27 El Niño has ended. The four levels are Hayo <27°C, Hinay 27–32°C, Hinga 33–41°C (or loss of school water supply) and Hinto ≥42°C. Every outlet that gives numbers agrees on these, and the bands are exactly PAGASA's heat-index categories (not hazardous / caution / extreme caution / danger), so the "unusually low" worry in the prior notes is resolved: they are not misreported. School heads choose the level, but in coordination with the Schools Division Office (SDO), and they must inform the Schools Division Superintendent (SDS). The memo asks for on-site indoor readings at 8:00, 12:00 and 14:00. In practice in Western Visayas (3 Nov 2026), suspensions were triggered by the PAGASA next-day forecast.

**Who decides**

- Schools may shift to alternative delivery modes **without an LGU declaration** if they inform the SDS. - "School principals or heads may automatically suspend classes when the forecast heat index reaches 42°C or higher." — search summaries [UNVERIFIED]

**Measurement: when and what**
- Indoor classroom temperature and humidity are monitored "at 8:00 a.m., 12:00 noon and 2:00 p.m." — [DepEdClub [read]](https://depedclub.com/deped-heat-guidelines-2026-4h-class-suspensions/) (secondary; the times are not confirmed in any mainstream outlet I could read)
- Schools must install calibrated thermometers with hygrometers so that classroom conditions are measured on site "not estimated from a city-wide forecast". — [astig.ph [read]](https://astig.ph/deped-memorandum-64-heat-class-suspension-42-degrees-2026/)
- "School heads will use these daily readings to determine the appropriate 4H learning modality." — [DepEd Tambayan [read]](https://www.depedtambayanph.net/2026/10/deped-memo-064-s-2026-el-nino-4h-scenario.html)
- **Forecast and day-before practice.** On 3 Nov 2026, 122 WV public schools (113,022 learners) suspended F2F classes on PAGASA forecasts. **SDO Iloilo City accounted for 65 schools and 73,127 learners**, on a forecast of 43°C ("danger"). Roxas City was at 42°C; Dumangas and Mambusao at 40°C. — [Daily Guardian [read]](https://www.dailyguardian.com.ph/blog/extreme-heat-suspends-classes-in-122-western-visayas-schools); [PNA [read]](https://www.pna.gov.ph/article/122-w-visayas-public-schools-suspend-in-person-classes-due-to-extreme-heat); [Philstar [read]](https://www.philstar.com/nation/2026/11/03/2567603/43c-extreme-heat-disrupts-classes-western-visayas)

- In Mandaue, school heads decide the next day's level "every afternoon from 4 p.m. to 5 p.m." using hygrometers plus daily forecasts, then notify parents through official channels and class group chats. Division officers monitor each school's heat index every 2–3 hours. — [SunStar Cebu [read]](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/deped-mandaue-implements-4-level-heat-index-framework-to-protect-students)

**Other DM 064 content**
- Safe drinking stations and water dispensers, immediate leak repair, and coordination with water concessionaires. Prioritise essential literacy, numeracy and core standards. Pre-position self-learning modules before the hottest months. — [astig.ph [read]](https://astig.ph/deped-memorandum-64-heat-class-suspension-42-degrees-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Encode this:** `level = f(heat index)` with HI <27 → HAYO, 27–32 → HINAY, 33–41 → HINGA, ≥42 → HINTO. Also force at least HINGA when the school has no water. Treat fractional values between 32 and 33 as HINAY (≤32.x) and between 41 and 42 as HINGA, since the published bands are integers.
- The memo's design is measured and same-day: indoor readings at 8/12/14. Actual WV practice, and the Mandaue 4–5 p.m. decision window, use the **PAGASA next-day forecast and a decision the afternoon before**. Presko's next-day recommendation fits that practice well. Present it as *decision support for the school head, to be confirmed by the SDO*, not as the decision.
- In Western Visayas, the 3 Nov 43°C forecast mapped to HINTO, which means no home-based work either. Presko's "ADM" output should therefore be shown only for HINGA, and HINTO should show "no academic activity".
- Iloilo classrooms run hotter indoors than the PAGASA outdoor forecast (assumption, unsourced). A forecast of 33°C or more alone would put most days at HINGA. Many Iloilo days are probably HINAY or HINGA on paper, so the framework's real bite is in how school heads pick between them.

### Gaps
- The primary memo text could not be read (403), so these are unconfirmed: whether the level is set by the indoor reading, the PAGASA forecast, or the worse of the two; whether the 8/12/2 times are in the memo itself; and any formal day-before notification rule.
- The "June 2026" predecessor framework (number and title) was not identified.
- Rules on make-up classes and on how long a HINGA spell may last before learning-recovery measures were not found.

## Q2. DO 014 s.2026, DO 22 s.2024 and DO 37 s.2022

### Takeaway
DO 22 s.2024, "Revised Guidelines on Class and Work Suspension in Schools during Disasters and Emergencies" (Dec 2024), is the base suspension order. It reportedly repeals DO 37 s.2022, so **do not cite DO 37 s.2022 as current**. DO 014 s.2026 ("Guidelines on Learning Continuity in Emergencies") is named by one explainer as the basis for the 4H framework, but its text was not read.

### Cited Findings
- DO 22 s.2024 covers extreme heat alongside typhoons, floods, earthquakes and power outages, and gives school heads more authority to suspend for localised factors. DO 37 s.2022 "repealed". — search summaries of [Manila Bulletin, 27 Dec 2024](https://mb.com.ph/2024/12/27/dep-ed-updates-class-suspension-guidelines), [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2019125/deped-revises-suspension-guidelines-to-address-learning-disruption/amp), [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/deped-mandaue-to-update-class-suspension-guidelines) [UNVERIFIED]; PDF at deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/DO_s2024_022.pdf (not read)

It was reiterated for heat in March 2025 by joint memorandum OM-OSEC-OUOPS-2025-01-01456 ([PDF via SDO Nueva Vizcaya](https://sdonuevavizcaya.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-03-14-JOINT-MEMORANDUM-OM-OSEC-OUOPS-2025-01-01456-REITERATION-OF-DO-22-s.-2024-ON-CLASS-SUSPENSION-AND-STRENGTHENED-MONITORING-AMID-INCREASED-HEAT-INDEX-LEVELS.pdf)) (from prior notes)

## Q3. The July 2026 draft EO (automatic suspension at ≥40°C): signed?

### Takeaway
Yes, in a changed form. **Executive Order No. 130, s.2026** was signed by President Marcos and amends EO 66 s.2012. The Daily Guardian says it was signed 28 Oct 2026 and made public 3 Nov; other outlets reported it around 3–4 Nov. It sets three tiers: **automatic** (on hazard warnings or advisories), **localized** (declared by the local chief executive) and **granular** (declared by the school after a site assessment). Learning continuity is required under each. No source I could read confirms that a specific 40°C heat-index automatic trigger survived into the signed text.

### Cited Findings
- EO 130 was signed by Marcos on 28 Oct 2026, made public 3 Nov, and amends EO No. 66 (s. 2012). Suspending in-person classes "does not necessarily mean suspending learning". — [Daily Guardian [read]](https://www.dailyguardian.com.ph/blog/extreme-heat-suspends-classes-in-122-western-visayas-schools)
- The three tiers: automatic "when specific criteria regarding weather or hazards are met"; localized, decided by local leaders; granular, where schools suspend when hazards are seen in their vicinity. "Suspension of in-person classes does not automatically mean a halt to learning". DepEd, CHED, TESDA and ECCDC issue guidelines for alternative delivery and continuity. — [Manila Times, 4 Nov 2026 [read]](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/04/news/national/palace-releases-class-suspension-guidelines/2460865)
- Schools may declare localized suspensions when there is no national or local order, if site conditions show "an actual or imminent threat". They may "automatically suspend on-site classes and work upon issuance of any warning, advisory, alert level, or forecast" from PAGASA, PHIVOLCS or DENR-EMB. Quezon City is waiting for the implementing guidelines. — [GMA News [read]](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/1008199/qc-aligns-heat-related-class-suspension-rules-with-deped-marcos-eo/story/)
- Agencies have 30 days from effectivity to issue implementing guidelines and technical thresholds. — search summary [UNVERIFIED]
- The July 2026 draft proposed automatic suspension at a heat index ≥40°C. School heads could recommend campus-specific suspensions, with SDS concurrence for public schools. — [Daily Tribune, 29 Jul 2026](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/07/29/deped-proposes-automatic-class-suspensions-at-40c-heat-index-hazard-alerts) (from prior notes)

### Inferences
- If the deadline runs from 28 Oct, the 30-day guidelines would fall due around late Nov 2026. As of 21 Nov 2026 they may not exist yet, so the numeric automatic heat trigger under EO 130 is **pending or unknown**. Presko should make this trigger configurable (default ≥42°C, consistent with DM 064 HINTO) and show "EO 130 implementing guidelines pending".

### Gaps
- The EO 130 text, its exact automatic heat threshold (40 vs 42°C), any announcement-timing rule and its resumption rules were not read. The Official Gazette was not fetched.

## Q4. Iloilo City EO No. 24 s.2025: still in force in 2026?

### Takeaway
EO 24 s.2025 (effective 9 Mar 2025) was still listed as an active city policy in a UN ESCAP / Iloilo City workshop report dated **24–25 July 2026**. I found **no revocation or amendment**, and no Quezon-City-style alignment with DM 064. Its thresholds are reported inconsistently:
- 40°C optional / 42°C automatic (GMA, Mar 2025);
- 41°C automatic in use (Apr 2025);
- automatic threshold "lowered from 42°C to 40°C" (ESCAP, Jul 2026).

The November 2026 Iloilo City suspensions were reported under DM 064 and school-head decisions, not under a city EO.

### Cited Findings
- EO 24 (effective 9 Mar 2025): 40°C is optional, for school heads and university presidents; 42°C is an automatic F2F suspension with a shift to ADM. The trigger is PAGASA's 5 p.m. advisory the day before. Schools may start early so classes end by noon, with afternoon classes on ADM. — [GMA Regional TV, 10 Mar 2025](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/106983/new-protocols-issued-on-class-suspension-in-iloilo-city-due-to-heat-index/story) (from prior notes); same 42°C automatic trigger in [Cebu Daily News](https://cebudailynews.inquirer.net/626998/iloilo-city-if-heat-index-hits-42-c-automatic-f2f-class-suspension) [UNVERIFIED snippet]
- 7 Apr 2025: suspension on a 41°C forecast, with the automatic threshold "lowered" to 41°C. — [GMA Regional TV, 7 Apr 2025](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/107479/iloilo-city-cancels-inperson-classes-due-to-41c-heat-index/story) (from prior notes; the amending instrument is not identified)
- ESCAP workshop report (24–25 July 2026), table of existing city policies: "New Heat Index Class Suspension Thresholds (Executive Order No. 024, Series of 2025): Established strict guidelines and a new heat index threshold (lowered from 42°C to 40°C) for the automatic suspension of face-to-face classes. Implemented by the Iloilo City Government." The same table lists EO 20 s.2025 (heat index advisories and temperature-regulated evacuation centres) and EO 023 s.2025 (urban greening task force). — [UN ESCAP meeting report PDF [read]](https://www.unescap.org/sites/default/d8files/event-documents/Meeting%20Report,%20Iloilo%20Urban%20Heat%20Action%20Planning%20Workshop,%2024-25%20June%202026.pdf)

- Quezon City revoked its MC No. 3 s.2025 to align with DM 064 and EO 130. — [GMA News [read]](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/1008199/qc-aligns-heat-related-class-suspension-rules-with-deped-marcos-eo/story/)
- Bacolod let schools decide on heat suspensions in Nov 2026, and Cebu City's mayor reviewed automatic heat-suspension rules on 4 Nov 2026. — [Panay News](https://www.panaynews.net/bacolod-schools-allowed-to-decide-on-class-suspensions-amid-extreme-heat/); [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/mayor-reviews-heat-class-rules) [UNVERIFIED: titles and snippets]

### Inferences
- Treat the city rule as an **LGU "localized" overlay** (EO 130 tier 2) on top of DM 064. Read the 5 p.m. PAGASA forecast the day before. If the forecast is ≥ the city's automatic threshold, city-wide F2F suspension applies. Make the threshold configurable (40/41/42); the default could be 42°C, matching both the original EO text and DM 064 HINTO, with a note that sources report 40 or 41. The "start early, end by noon" option maps to DM 064 HINAY (shortened day, morning-only).
- The ESCAP "42 → 40" phrasing may conflate the optional 40°C tier with the automatic tier. It conflicts with GMA's March 2025 reporting.

### Gaps
- No Iloilo City EO amending or revoking EO 24 s.2025 was found, and no instrument for the 41°C figure was identified. The city's official site and the SP records were not searched.
- Whether Mayor Raisa Treñas (in office since mid-2025) has issued any 2026 heat EO is unknown.

### Inferences

### Gaps
