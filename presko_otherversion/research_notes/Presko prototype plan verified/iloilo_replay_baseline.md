## Which replay date is best, and what is N?

### Takeaway
**Use Monday, April 1, 2024.** It is the only date with a sourced, citywide school count. DepEd data (as of 3 p.m., April 1) shows **66 Iloilo City schools** suspended in-person classes. That equals the SDO's full public-school count of 66, so it was a citywide public-school closure under city EO No. 047. The PAGASA forecast was **41°C** for April 1 ("danger" per the EO), with 42–43°C for April 2. No sourced learner count exists for Iloilo City for any day. The learner figure has to be an estimate from enrolment and labelled as one.

The runner-up is **April 25, 2024** (PAGASA forecast 43°C, no EO, a snippet-only "67 public schools"). **April 30, 2024** is notable: PAGASA forecast 44°C for Iloilo City, and DepEd's nationwide public-school ADM order covered April 29–30.

### Inferences
- **N for April 1:** 66 schools (sourced). This matches TeacherPH's count of 66 public schools for SY 2024-25 (see baseline), so it was effectively every SDO Iloilo City public school. Learners: **no sourced city figure**. The best proxy is SDO public enrolment, about 76,000 (TeacherPH SY 2024-25 public sum of 75,890). SY 2023-24 enrolment was not found. Show this as "≈76k learners (estimate from enrolment)", not as a reported count.
- **The prior notes were wrong on one point:** April 29–30 *was* a suspension day for Iloilo City public schools, through the DepEd nationwide order (inferred: "all public schools nationwide" includes SDO Iloilo City; no Iloilo-specific confirmation found). PAGASA forecast 44°C for April 30, the highest Iloilo City forecast of the April dates checked, so the earlier Open-Meteo proxy (39.4°C) reads about 4.6°C low on that day.
- For April 1–5 the replay framing "city EO ordered closure; school heads shifted to ADM" is solid. For April 25–26, the framing is "mayor announced; school heads of 67 public schools implemented (UNVERIFIED)".
- 66 vs 67 public schools: the count may differ by date or definition (e.g. one annex or newly separated school). The source is not resolved.

## SDO Iloilo City baseline

### Takeaway
SDO Iloilo City has **66 public schools** (174 schools including private and SUC/LUC), about **75,900 public learners** (SY 2024-25), **2,980 public teachers** and **2,242 public classrooms**. All of these come from TeacherPH's compilation of DepEd public school data as of Oct 25, 2025. In SY 2024-25, **18 public schools ran shifting schedules**. Only 3 of the 18 are named in sources.

### Cited Findings
- [read] SY 2024-25 (DepEd data as of Oct 25, 2025, via TeacherPH): 174 schools in total (66 public, 105 private, 3 SUC/LUC), plus 73 ALS CLCs. The page also says "65 reported data". Learners: 109,985 total (107,999 in school + 1,986 ALS). By sector: **public 75,890**, private 30,586, SUC/LUC 1,396 ("at least" figures). Public schools: **2,980 teachers** (1,673 elementary, 1,018 JHS, 289 SHS per the search summary), **345 non-teaching staff**, **2,242 classrooms**. Curricular offerings (all sectors, not public-only): elementary only 97, K–10 26, K–12 24, JHS only 1, JHS+SHS 17, SHS only 9. Districts (schools / learners / public teachers): Arevalo 14/10,625/367; City Proper 26/22,268/366; Jaro 51/28,454/701; Lapaz 26/12,502/351; Mandurriao 25/11,165/372; Molo 21/15,744/547; Montes-Lapuz 11/7,241/276. — [TeacherPH, Iloilo City division](https://www.teacherph.com/schools/divisions/iloilo-city) (third-party aggregator of DepEd data; not checked against DepEd's own portal)
- UNVERIFIED (snippet): SY 2025-26 initial enrolment of 86,485 (DepEd-6). — [Manila Bulletin, Jun 16, 2025](https://mb.com.ph/2025/06/16/enrollment-in-western-visayas-public-schools-drops). This exceeds TeacherPH's public count, so it may include private schools.

No full list, enrolment or shortage figure is given. — [IMT News, Aug 1, 2024](https://imtnews.ph/18-public-schools-in-iloilo-city-implement-shifting-schedules-due-to-classroom-shortage)

- UNVERIFIED (snippet, date unknown): Iloilo City classroom deficit of about 482 (Regional Classroom Master Plan). — [Panay News](https://www.panaynews.net/?p=431993)

### Inferences
- 66 public schools (TeacherPH) = 66 schools suspended on April 1, 2024 (GMA). The April 1 replay can show "66 of 66 public schools".
- Public learners per classroom ≈ 75,890 / 2,242 ≈ 34. The division average hides the crowding at big secondary schools (ICNHS is about 39 learners per room even before splitting into shifts).
- ICNHS Grades 9–10 attend 1:10–8:20 p.m., so they sit through peak afternoon heat. This is a concrete hook for Presko's shift-aware planning.

### Gaps
- Public-only breakdown into elementary / secondary / integrated was not found (TeacherPH's offering counts mix sectors).
- The names of the other 15 shifting schools were not found.
- No official SDO Iloilo City figure for the classroom shortage (482 is snippet-only and undated).
- No SY 2023-24 (the April 2024 school year) enrolment for SDO Iloilo City.
