# Presko pitch: verified evidence figures and parent-messaging norms

Research date: 21 Nov 2026. This builds on, and corrects, the notes in `research_notes/Presko prototype research plan/`. **[UNVERIFIED]** means I saw only a search snippet or summary and could not read the page in full (Inquirer, Rappler and PIA returned 403 during this session).

## 1. World Bank learning poverty for the Philippines: 91%, 69.5% and "67%"

### Takeaway
Quote it as: **"91% of Filipino 10-year-olds cannot read and understand a simple text (World Bank, 2019 data)."** 91% (90.9%) is a **2019 data-year** figure based on SEA-PLM 2019, which the World Bank first published in its **June 2022** update. It is not a 2022 measurement. The **69.5%** figure is an older pre-COVID estimate for 2019 that the World Bank used before SEA-PLM results were in its series; it is now superseded. The **"67%"** is not a Philippine rate. It is the "two-thirds" threshold in a Sept 2023 East Asia & Pacific report. I found no later Philippine figure.

### Cited Findings
- World Bank Human Capital brief for the Philippines (2025, primary source, text extracted from the PDF): "**Learning poverty. In 2019, 91% of children could not read and understand a simple text, by age 10.** The indicator is higher than the regional average." The brief "uses the latest available data", so 2019 is still the latest data year in 2025. — [World Bank Philippines brief (2025)](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/f7ba8fbb2c367f7f0089339b3c2b6f78-0140012025/original/Philippines.pdf)
- Coverage of the World Bank's June 2022 "State of Global Learning Poverty" update gives **90.9%** for the Philippines before the pandemic, from 2019 estimates. Its components: **learning deprivation of 90.4%** (end-of-primary children reading below minimum proficiency), about **5% out of school**, and boys 91.7% vs girls 89.2% below minimum proficiency. **[UNVERIFIED: search summary of Inquirer; page 403]** — [Inquirer, "WB: PH 'learning poverty' among highest in region"](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1632864/wb-ph-learning-poverty-among-highest-in-region)
- The data source behind 91%: in SEA-PLM 2019 (Grade 5), only **10%** of Filipino pupils met the minimum reading standard and **17%** met the minimum mathematics standard. — [SEA-PLM 2019 National Report of the Philippines](https://neqmap.bangkok.unesco.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Southeast-Asia-Primary-Learning-Metrics-2019-National-Report-of-the-Philippines.pdf); [UNICEF PH Education Factsheet](https://www.unicef.org/philippines/media/7386/file/Education%20Factsheet.pdf) **[figures from search summary]**
- **69.5%:** Inquirer coverage of a Nov 2021 World Bank report on remote learning said that "in 2019, or before COVID-19 struck, learning poverty in the Philippines was already 69.5 percent" and had risen to "as much as 90 percent" by Aug 2021 (World Bank simulation). It also cited an EAP regional average of 34.5%. **[UNVERIFIED: snippet; page 403]** — [Inquirer Business, "WB: Remote learning jacked up learning poverty in PH to 90% in 2021"](https://business.inquirer.net/334736/wb-remote-learning-jacked-up-learning-poverty-in-ph-to-90-in-2021/amp)
- **"67%":** Inquirer (22 Sept 2023), on the World Bank EAP report, said the Philippines was among eight countries above two-thirds (about 67%). The World Bank's press release says only that learning poverty is above 50% in 14 of 22 middle-income EAP countries. **[UNVERIFIED: snippets]** — [Inquirer, "PH 'learning poverty' still among region's worst"](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1835164/ph-learning-poverty-still-among-regions-worst); [World Bank press release, 20/21 Sept 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2023/09/20/improving-learning-outcomes-key-to-achieving-development-aspirations-in-middle-income-east-asia-and-pacific-countries-wo)

### Inferences
- The 69.5% figure was probably a pre-SEA-PLM estimate that relied on older or partial data. The 2022 update re-based the 2019 value at 90.9% once SEA-PLM 2019 was added. Using "69.5% → 90% → 91%" as a trend would be misleading. **Do not show it as a trend.**
- Safe slide wording: "9 in 10 Filipino 10-year-olds can't read a simple text (World Bank; 2019 data, latest available)."

### Gaps
- I could not open the June 2022 Philippines Learning Poverty Brief itself. The 90.9 / 90.4 / ~5% split comes from press coverage.
- I could not read the original Nov 2021 World Bank document to confirm the methodology behind the 69.5% figure.

## 2. UNICEF "Learning Interrupted" (Jan 2025): global figures and the Philippines

### Takeaway
All the headline numbers are confirmed from UNICEF primary pages: **at least 242 million students in 85 countries/territories (2024); heatwaves were the top hazard at 171 million; 74% of affected students were in low- and lower-middle-income countries.** Two more figures: **118 million in April 2024 alone** (Bangladesh, Cambodia, India, the Philippines and Thailand combined) and **50 million in East Asia & Pacific**. UNICEF gives **no Philippines-only student count** in the press release or on the report landing page.

### Cited Findings
- "At least 242 million students" in 85 countries or territories had schooling disrupted by climate events in 2024. Heatwaves affected "an estimated 171 million students". "74 per cent of the 242 million affected students" were in low- and lower-middle-income countries. At least 1 in 7 students was affected. South Asia was hardest hit with 128 million. The report covers pre-primary to upper secondary. — [UNICEF report page, "Learning Interrupted: Global Snapshot of Climate-Related School Disruptions in 2024"](https://www.unicef.org/reports/learning-interrupted-global-snapshot-2024); [PDF](https://www.unicef.org/media/170626/file/Global-snapshot-climate-related-school-disruptions-2024.pdf)
- The press release (dateline **24 January 2025**; the page header shows 23 Jan) says: "over 118 million students affected in April alone"; "almost 74 per cent of affected students last year were in low and lower-middle income countries"; "in East Asia and the Pacific, 50 million students' schooling was affected"; "Bangladesh and the Philippines experienced widespread school closures in April." — [UNICEF press release](https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/nearly-quarter-billion-childrens-schooling-was-disrupted-climate-crises-2024-unicef)
- Typhoon Yagi affected 16 million children in EAP and was the top hazard in September 2024. This is a regional figure. — [UNICEF report page](https://www.unicef.org/reports/learning-interrupted-global-snapshot-2024)

### Inferences
- The release wording is "almost 74 per cent" and the report wording is "74 per cent". Both are fine to quote.
- For a Philippine figure, use DepEd's own 2024 counts (section 3) instead of inventing a UNICEF number.

### Gaps
- I did not read the full 4 MB PDF's country annex. It may contain a Philippine estimate, so check it before claiming "none".

## 4. How Iloilo / Western Visayas schools tell parents about class changes

### Takeaway
The documented channels are **LGU executive orders posted on the City Government / Mayor's Facebook pages**, the **Mayor's statements by Viber to media** (2024), and **local radio / news re-reporting**, notably Bombo Radyo Iloilo in Hiligaynon. Under DM 064 s.2026 (4H), school heads choose the level and, at least in Mandaue, **must inform teachers, learners and parents at least a day before**. Nationally, DepEd tells parents to trust **only official DepEd/LGU channels** because fake "Walang Pasok" Facebook pages keep appearing. I found **no source documenting** Iloilo school use of SMS blasts or class Messenger group chats. They are widely assumed but **unverified**.

### Cited Findings
- Bombo Radyo Iloilo (4 Nov 2026, in Hiligaynon) quoted Atty. Russel Tabang, Legal Officer of **SDO Iloilo City**, on the 4H implementation in the city. At 27°C heat index classes continue. At 33–41°C, F2F classes are automatically suspended and move to ADM ("modular kag online"). At 42°C classes are automatically suspended. At 27–32°C, hybrid should be considered. Schools may also ask to bring back F2F. The article does not say how parents are told. — [Bombo Radyo Iloilo](https://iloilo.bomboradyo.com/mga-eskwelahan-sa-siyudad-sang-iloilo-otomatiko-nga-ma-suspend-ang-klase-kun-maglab-ot-sa-42-degrees-celsius-ang-heat-index-legal-officer-sang-schools-division-of-iloilo-city/)
- Early Nov 2026: Iloilo schools suspended or adjusted classes on a forecast 43°C heat index, which is Level 4 "Hinto" (≥42°C, suspending both F2F and modular/asynchronous requirements). **[UNVERIFIED: snippet; PIA 403]** — [PIA](https://pia.gov.ph/news/visayas/wv/schools-suspend-adjust-classes-as-43c-heat-index-raises-safety-concerns-in-iloilo/); see also [Inquirer, "Western Visayas schools suspend classes due to 43°C heat index"](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2335171/western-visayas-schools-suspend-classes-due-to-43c-heat-index) and [Panay News, "113,022 students across WV hit by heat-triggered class suspensions"](https://www.panaynews.net/113022-students-across-wv-hit-by-heat-triggered-class-suspensions/) (titles only; pages 403/redirect)
- The city announces through its official Facebook page. Example: a post on "the official Facebook page of the government of Iloilo City" ordering suspension after a 4.8 quake. — [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1050467/classes-in-iloilo-city-suspended-after-4-8-magnitude-quake/amp) **[snippet]**
- 24 Apr 2024: Mayor Treñas told media by Viber he was "constrained to suspend all classes at all levels" for 25–26 April. — [Manila Times, 26 Apr 2024](https://manilatimes.net/2024/04/26/regions/classes-halted-anew-in-iloilo-city-schools/1943313) (from prior notes)
- EO 24 (2025) ties decisions to PAGASA's **5 p.m. heat-index advisory the day before**, so announcements happen in the evening. — [GMA Regional TV, 10 Mar 2025](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/106983/new-protocols-issued-on-class-suspension-in-iloilo-city-due-to-heat-index/story)
- 2026 city suspensions are issued as Mayor **Raisa Treñas** EOs, for example EO No. 007 s.2026 for Dinagyang. — [search summary of Iloilo coverage] **[UNVERIFIED]**; [Manila Bulletin, 22 Feb 2026](https://mb.com.ph/2026/02/22/iloilo-city-schools-suspend-classes-for-people-power-anniversary-asean-meetings)
- Under 4H, school heads choose the classification and inform teachers, learners and parents at least a day before classes (Mandaue example). — [Philstar, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.philstar.com/nation/2026/11/13/2569839/deped-adopts-4h-heat-guidelines) (from prior notes, snippet)
- DepEd says official announcements go only on "DepEd Philippines' official social media accounts and website". It has repeatedly disowned fake "Walang Pasok Advisory" Facebook pages (2017; fact-check Mar 2026) and an AI-generated fake suspension video. — [Manila Bulletin, 12 Mar 2026](https://mb.com.ph/2026/03/12/deped-warns-public-vs-fake-post-claiming-thursdayfriday-class-suspension); [Philstar 2017](https://philstar.com/headlines/2017/11/21/1760984/deped-disowns-walang-pasok-advisory); [MindaNews fact-check, Mar 2026](https://mindanews.com/top-stories/2026/03/fact-check-walang-pasok-advisory-page-spreads-misleading-class-suspension-claims/); [GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/953986/deped-warns-public-vs-fake-ai-generated-class-suspension-video/story)
- A division office (Angeles City) urged parents to monitor official DepEd and LGU channels. — [SunStar Pampanga](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/pampanga/public-urged-to-follow-official-class-suspension-guidelines) **[snippet]**

### Inferences
- Parent messages in Presko should (a) name the official source (SDO Iloilo City / school head and the PAGASA 5 p.m. advisory), (b) go out the evening before, and (c) be easy to paste into the school's own Facebook page and class group chats. Fake suspension posts are a known problem, so a "verified source" line is a selling point.
- Channel mix (Facebook page, Messenger/Viber class GCs, SMS for parents without data, radio pickup) is a reasonable design assumption. Present it as an assumption to validate with SDO Iloilo City, not as documented fact.

### Gaps
- No DepEd order specifically on *how* schools must notify parents was retrieved. DO 22 s.2024 and DM 064 s.2026 PDFs were not read.
- No source documents SMS or group-chat use by Iloilo schools, and RMN Iloilo was not checked.

## 5. Hiligaynon phrasing and code-switching in suspension announcements

### Takeaway
One real Hiligaynon text was verified: Bombo Radyo Iloilo, 4 Nov 2026. Its wording is **"ma-suspend ang klase"**, **"suspended ang face-to-face classes"**, **"padayon sa gihapon ang klase"** and **"magapadayon ang klase paagi sa alternative learning nga amo ang modular kag online"**. It **code-switches heavily**: the technical and policy terms stay in English (heat index, degrees Celsius, face-to-face, modular, online, hybrid, implementing guidelines). Official LGU and DepEd posts and executive orders are in **English**. Hiligaynon appears mainly in radio and news. I could not verify "Wala klase" or "suspendido ang klase" in a real announcement.

### Cited Findings
- Verbatim from Bombo Radyo Iloilo (4 Nov 2026, Bombo Shaine Toleran):
  1. "Otomatiko nga ma-suspend ang klase sa mga eskwelahan sa siyudad sang Iloilo kun maglab-ot sa 42 degrees Celsius"
  2. "Ginklaro ni Atty. Tabang nga kun 27 degrees Celsius ang heat index, padayon sa gihapon ang klase"
  3. "Sa 33-41 degrees Celsius naman, otomatiko nga suspended ang face-to-face classes"
  4. "magapadayon ang klase paagi sa alternative learning nga amo ang modular kag online."
  5. "Samtang, sa amo nga order, mahimo man nga magpangabay ang mga eskwelahan nga ibalik ang face-to-face classes"
  — [Bombo Radyo Iloilo](https://iloilo.bomboradyo.com/mga-eskwelahan-sa-siyudad-sang-iloilo-otomatiko-nga-ma-suspend-ang-klase-kun-maglab-ot-sa-42-degrees-celsius-ang-heat-index-legal-officer-sang-schools-division-of-iloilo-city/)
- Official instruments (EO 047 series 2024, EO 24 2025, EO 007 s.2026) are reported in English, and the Mayor's 2024 Viber statement was in English ("constrained to suspend all classes at all levels"). — [Daily Guardian](https://www.dailyguardian.com.ph/blog/iloilo-city-extends-f2f-class-halts-includes-colleges); [Manila Times](https://manilatimes.net/2024/04/26/regions/classes-halted-anew-in-iloilo-city-schools/1943313)
- National shorthand is the Tagalog "Walang pasok". GMA tags Iloilo suspensions under "Walang Pasok". — [GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/regions/389989/walang-pasok-classes-up-to-high-school-suspended-in-iloilo-city/story)

### Inferences
- A Presko template consistent with the verified register (my composition, **not** a real announcement):
  - Hinga / ADM: "PAHIBALO: Base sa PAGASA 5 PM heat index forecast (43°C), suspended ang face-to-face classes sa [School] buwas, [petsa]. Magapadayon ang klase paagi sa modular/online. — [School Head], SDO Iloilo City"
  - Half-day: "Buwas, [petsa], may face-to-face classes hasta 12:00 NN lang; ang hapon, modular." ("hasta / lang" usage is plausible but not verified)
  - Full day: "Padayon ang klase buwas, [petsa]. Magdala sang tubig kag payong."
- Keep "face-to-face", "modular", "online" and "heat index" in English, as Bombo does. Pair each message with an English version, which matches official practice.
- "Wala klase" is plausible colloquial Hiligaynon, but no source in this session showed it. Have a native speaker or SDO staff check every template.

### Gaps
- I did not find 2–3 real school or SDO Facebook announcement texts. Facebook is not readable via fetch and news rarely reproduces school posts. Only the Bombo text is verified.
- No source on official code-switching norms for the Iloilo LGU or DepEd.

## 6. Competitors and comparables, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
There is one significant **new comparable**: **DepEd Project LIGTAS+**, launched nationally on **26 Aug 2026**. It is an AI-enabled multi-hazard analytics and risk platform from DepEd DRRMS and ECAIR, aimed at **school-level** suspension and resumption decisions. Coverage does not confirm heat-index forecasting, next-day plans or parent messaging, and the school-level suspension recommendations were still "being reviewed". DepEd also pushed a "new hazard assessment tool" in Sept 2026, and a May 2026 platform launch was reported. I found no tool that gives a per-school, forecast-based next-day heat plan with drafted parent messages. Pitch Presko as **complementary to LIGTAS+** (the heat module, the decision and the parent message layer), not as a first-of-its-kind tool in every respect.

### Cited Findings
- Project LIGTAS+ (Learning Institution Geohazard Tracking and Assessment for Safety): national launch in Makati on 26 Aug 2026, built by DepEd DRRMS with the Education Center for AI Research (ECAIR). It consolidates hazard information and school-level risk data. Usec. Malcolm Garma said the aim is "more granular data, more science-based, more data-based decision-making" so that suspensions and resumptions can be decided per school. He cited heat index only as an example basis and added: "The recommendations are still being reviewed, however." There is no mention of parent messaging. — [GMA News, 26 Aug 2026](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/scitech/technology/999947/deped-s-project-ligtas-aims-for-school-level-class-suspensions-resumptions/story/)
- One report says LIGTAS+ includes a dashboard section for forecasts and hazard data for school administrators and LGUs. **[UNVERIFIED: snippet]** — [PTV News](https://ptvnews.ph/?p=251900)
- 2 Sept 2026: Angara urged schools to "utilize new hazard assessment tool to manage class disruptions". **[UNVERIFIED: title/snippet]** — [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2297675/angara-utilize-new-hazard-assessment-tool-to-manage-class-disruptions)
- 8 May 2026: "DepEd launches platform to prepare schools amid natural calamities". **[UNVERIFIED: title only; may be an earlier LIGTAS stage]** — [BusinessWorld](https://www.bworldonline.com/the-nation/2026/05/08/748485/deped-launches-platform-to-prepare-schools-amid-natural-calamities/)
- From prior notes (not re-checked): DM 064 s.2026 4H framework with manual indoor readings at 8/12/2; Iloilo City EO 24 (40/42°C, later 41°C); CDRI Heat Smart Schools guidance (2025, cites Arup/UCL UHeat); BDRCS heatwave EAP (not schools). I found no startup or UNICEF per-school heat tool. — see `research_notes/Presko prototype research plan/tools_programmes_adm_costs.md`

### Inferences
- Reframe the claim "no such tool exists" as: "DepEd's LIGTAS+ is building school-level hazard data. Presko is the missing heat-specific, next-day, 4H-mapped plan plus the parent message, and it could plug into LIGTAS+." This is also the integration path: DRRMS owns both.
- Note the threshold mismatch: SDO Iloilo City's legal officer described 33–41°C as automatic F2F suspension (4H "Hinga"), while EO 24 is 40/41–42°C. Presko should apply DM 064's 4H bands and show the LGU EO as an override.

### Gaps
- No LIGTAS+ technical documentation was found, and it is unclear whether it ingests PAGASA heat-index forecasts.
- Outside the Philippines, I ran no new searches in this session for 2025–2026 startup or LGU heat-school tools beyond the prior notes.
