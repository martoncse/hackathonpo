**SNT adoption:** 38,000 producers engaged two weeks after launch ([Koaci](https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/09/15/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-38-000-producteurs-deja-engages-dans-le-nouveau-systeme-de-tracabilite-du-cacao_200543.html)); 26,000 t bought through it in one week ([AIP](https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-26-000-tonnes-de-cacao-achetees-en-une-semaine-via-le-systeme-national-de-tracabilite/)).

**What may still be open:** the SNT and the card tie a sale to a registered producer. Whether the SNT checks if the volume sold is plausible for the producer's plots: **not found** (not confirmed absent). Large traders run their own yield controls. Neither produces the importer's Art 10/11 file. EFI's finding that paper exclusion doesn't keep cocoa out of the chain is the problem that a volume-plausibility check addresses.

## 5. Rules for the app (leads)

- **Volume ceiling:** 924 kg dry beans/ha = 2× the 462 kg/ha 2023/24 average. Review only, never exclusion: well-managed (pruned) farms can approach it, and carried-over stock (~200,000 t unsold in early 2026, [AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202602280103.html)) can inflate one season's sales. The flag text names both.
- **Whisp:** the BNETD 2020 land cover map (10 m, with a cocoa class, CC-BY-4.0) and a national cocoa probability layer exist ([Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/BNETD_land_cover_v1)). Whisp exposes them as `nCI_Forest_bnetd_2020` and `nCI_Cocoa_bnetd` ("for Côte d'Ivoire only", [Whisp layer list](https://github.com/forestdatapartnership/whisp/blob/main/layers_description.md)). The app sends `nationalCodes: ["ci"]`.

## 6–9. Legality, intake, outputs (as built)

- **Intake:** plots keyed by producer card (`producer_card`); one card can hold several plots. Deliveries are cooperative purchase records in kg of dry beans, season `2025-26` (main and mid crop pooled). Reading producer cards or SNT records isn't built: no importer data access found.
- **Rules:** `checkProducerVolumes` pools sales per card + crop over one season (the card's latest) and the area of all those plots (measured boundary, else declared); a card with sales but no area goes to review. Threshold 924 kg/ha (profile `rules.cocoa`). The 4 ha point/polygon rule is unchanged (Art 2(28)). Whisp field: `risk_pcrop` (cocoa is a perennial crop). `nationalCodes: ["ci"]` is sent (BNETD layers).
- **Outputs:** the same pack as Colombia, with `ProducerCountry: CI`, HS 1801 (`180100`), the producer card on each Art 9 plot, and Art 10 rows a, c, d, e, f, h replaced by Ivorian text from `profiles/cocoa-ci/profile.json`.
- **Demo data:** fully synthetic (names, cards, receipts, coordinates near Soubré); not checked with Whisp.

**Data protection:** Law 2013-450, administered by ARTCI. Every processing of personal data needs prior declaration or authorisation, which extends to geolocation systems and transfers abroad. A "third country" means outside ECOWAS. Hosting outside ECOWAS counts as a transfer to a third country and needs ARTCI authorisation; detailed transfer criteria: **not found** ([ARTCI via CIO Mag](https://cio-mag.com/?p=38189), [Journal officiel](https://cms21.anac.ci/uploads/748534/LOI_2013-450.pdf)).

## Final positioning

A verification layer on top of the CCC's national traceability system, not a competitor to it. The producer card ties a sale to a registered producer. EUDR-Snap checks whether a card's season sales are plausible for all its plots (review above 924 kg/ha, never exclusion), and drafts the Art 9/10/11 file for EU importers outside the large traders' in-house yield-control programmes.
