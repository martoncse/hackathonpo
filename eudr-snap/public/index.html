◆ EUDR-Snap open infrastructure for smallholder cooperatives
Whisp: not run

EU Deforestation Regulation applies to large and medium operators on 30 December 2026

– days

From then, coffee and cocoa entering the EU must be traced to plots not deforested after 2020. Colombia has more than 500,000 coffee families. Plots are often mapped already (FNC's SICA). In areas with limited internet or electricity, deliveries are often recorded on paper (to confirm with a cooperative). EUDR-Snap links receipts to plots, and flags volumes above what the plot normally produces, for review.
