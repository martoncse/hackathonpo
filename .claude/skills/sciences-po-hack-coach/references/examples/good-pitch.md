# Worked example: DossierClair (pitch)

The opening, run of show, video script and Q&A for the DossierClair spec in `good-spec.md`. Use it as a shape reference.

---

## A. The opening

**1. The wedge:** *"A third of households eligible for the RSA don't claim it, often because of paperwork."*
**15 words.** One figure, one cause.

**2. The move:** *"We built DossierClair, which turns three photos of documents into a benefits action sheet for social workers."*
**17 words.** One name, one verb, one user.

**3. The moment:** *"Watch this."*
Then the demo: three photos in, the sheet out. About 40 seconds. Don't talk over it except to name what's on screen.

---

## B. The 5-minute run of show

| Time | Slide | What we say |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 0:00–0:30 | Problem | The wedge. "Meet Karima, a social worker in a CCAS. She has 30 minutes per household." DREES 2022 on screen |
| 0:30–1:45 | Solution | The move, "Watch this", live demo on the synthetic household |
| 1:45–2:15 | Why better | Today: a paper checklist and the online simulator, typed in by hand. DossierClair reads the documents directly |
| 2:15–2:50 | Scale | Every commune with a CCAS is a potential user; start with one department |
| 2:50–3:20 | Impact | Benefits opened per 100 appointments, and minutes saved on each one |
| 3:20–4:30 | Rollout | Three-month pilot with one CCAS, funded from its digital budget; the department scales it. Documents not stored; GDPR impact assessment before real use |
| 4:30–5:00 | Close | "We're looking for one CCAS to pilot with. After that: all 15 national benefits." |

Rehearsed with a timer: **4:40**.

## D. Jury Q&A

### The standard five, public policy lens

1. **Who owns this inside the administration on day one?**
   > A CCAS director running a three-month pilot with five social workers.
2. **How is it funded, and is it cheaper than what exists?**
   > The pilot fits in a CCAS digital budget, and it saves social-worker time that today goes on manual checks.
3. **What happens when the AI gets it wrong for a citizen?**
   > The social worker sees the source document behind every line and makes the decision; the tool never files anything.
4. **What about GDPR and sensitive data?**
   > Documents are processed during the appointment and not stored, and a GDPR impact assessment comes before any real use.
5. **How would you measure that it worked?**
   > Benefits opened per 100 appointments and minutes spent on documents, before versus during the pilot.

### Jury-specific

- **Véronique Mathelin (Impact Studio): who concretely benefits?**
  > The households who leave with a benefit they would have missed, counted in the pilot indicator.
- **Nour Mikhael (Google, safety): what if someone uploads fake documents?**
  > It reads documents; it doesn't verify them. The CAF's own checks still apply when the claim is filed.
- **Fabrizio Nastri (FlexUp): is there a business here?**
  > Yes, licensing to departments, but we're pitching the public pilot first because that's where the proof comes from.
