# Hackathon Coaching Session — Crafting the Pitch

## Problem/Feature Description

A team at the Sciences Po AI Hackathon 2026 has completed Phases 1–3. Their spec and plan are locked.

**Project:** PlanCheck Climat — a municipal officer uploads the town's climate plan and the tool checks it against the legal obligations, highlighting missing measures with page references.

**Lens:** Public policy

**Demo moment (from spec):**
- Juror does: uploads a real, public 80-page municipal climate plan (PDF)
- Our product does: checks it against the list of mandatory sections and measures
- Juror sees: a checklist with green ticks, three red gaps, and the page number for each finding

**Adoption (from spec):** pilot with one intercommunality's climate officer; funded from the territory's engineering-support budget.

Camille, the team's storyteller, opens Phase 4:

> "OK let's write the pitch. I was thinking: 'Climate change is one of the most important challenges of our time, and local authorities have a crucial role to play in the ecological transition, which is why we built PlanCheck Climat, an innovative AI-powered platform leveraging cutting-edge language models to revolutionise the way territories approach their climate planning obligations. Watch this.' And then we spend most of the five minutes on why climate matters. Good?"

You are the hackathon coaching assistant. Run Phase 4.

## Output Specification

Conduct the Phase 4 session and produce `session-log.md` with the full conversation, generating Camille's replies as you go (she will resist cutting at first, then cooperate). Continue until the Phase 4 exit conditions are met and the terminal state is reached.

Format each turn as:

```
## Coach
[message]

## Camille
[reply]
```

Also produce `pitch.md` containing:
1. The three opening sentences, with word counts
2. A 5-minute run of show mapped to the six slides, with timings
3. A demo-video shot list under 2 minutes
4. The five public-policy Q&A pairs and at least two jury-specific ones, one sentence each
5. A dry-run checklist with all items checked
