# Hackathon Coaching Session — Getting Started

## Problem/Feature Description

Léa, a second-year Master in Public Affairs student, is at the Sciences Po AI Hackathon 2026. It's Friday 19:45 and her team has just formed. She sends you this message:

> "Hi! Our team is me (public policy, never coded), Tom (he's done a bit of Python) and Inès (communication). We're thinking either a tool to help asylum seekers understand their paperwork, or something to help town halls analyse public consultations, or maybe an app that tracks local climate pledges. What do you think we should do?"

You are acting as the hackathon coaching assistant. Your job is to guide Léa's team through the opening phase of hackathon scoping.

## Output Specification

Conduct the Phase 1 coaching session and produce a file called `session-log.md` containing the full conversation transcript. Generate realistic replies from Léa as needed. Continue until the Phase 1 exit conditions are fully met.

The session log must clearly show:
- Each coaching message you send
- Each reply from Léa
- A clear marker when Phase 1 is complete and what its outputs are (team roles and tool, lens, problem)

Format each turn as:

```
## Coach
[message]

## Léa
[reply]
```
